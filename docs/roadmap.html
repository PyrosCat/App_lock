Already delivered

The v1.0.0 core, built

The protection at the heart of v1.0.0 is implemented today, and its core locking behaviour is checked by an automated regression gate on real hardware.

PIN & biometric locking

Passcodes are hashed with Argon2id at OWASP-recommended strength and verified in constant time. Biometric unlock with a PIN fallback.

Brute-force defense

Escalating lockout with persisted counters after repeated failures, plus a watchdog that alerts if protection is switched off.

Encrypted at rest

Credentials and local data are sealed with Argon2id, SQLCipher, and AES-256-GCM under the Android Keystore — encrypted from first launch.

Validated on real hardware

Core locking passes an automated regression gate twice over on a physical Moto G (Android 15) and across an emulator matrix from Android 8 to 15.

Modern engineering

Continuous integration, automated code-quality gates, dependency governance, and a dependency-injected, layered architecture.

Local-first & private

No accounts, no cloud, no analytics. Everything stays on the device — nothing about your protected apps ever leaves the phone.

The v2.0.0 horizon

The fuller vision, next

Deliberately held back so v1.0.0 can ship focused and fully verified. Several of these are already prototyped and reserved for the v2.0.0 line.

Private encrypted vault

Move photos and files into a private vault sealed with AES-256-GCM over SQLCipher, with byte-identical export and secure delete.

Prototyped · v2.0.0

Intruder capture

After repeated failed unlocks, a silent front-camera photo stored encrypted, with a timestamped record for review.

Prototyped · v2.0.0

Smart automation

Schedules and Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, or location triggers that lock and unlock protected apps automatically.

Planned · v2.0.0

Faster optional detection

An opt-in accessibility enhancement for lower-latency locking, layered on the Play-compliant baseline for users who want it.
