Already delivered

Built and validated

The protection users rely on is implemented today — and its core locking behaviour is checked by an automated regression gate on real hardware.

PIN & biometric locking

Passcodes are hashed with Argon2id at OWASP-recommended strength and verified in constant time. Biometric unlock with a PIN fallback.

Encrypted vault

Move photos and files into a private vault sealed with AES-256-GCM over a SQLCipher database. Byte-identical export and secure delete.

Intruder capture

After repeated failed unlock attempts, a silent front-camera photo is taken and stored encrypted, with a timestamped record for review.

Brute-force defense

Escalating lockout with persisted counters after repeated failures, plus a watchdog that alerts if protection is switched off.

Validated on real hardware

Core locking passes an automated regression gate twice over on a physical Moto G (Android 15) and across an emulator matrix from Android 8 to 15.

Modern engineering

Continuous integration, automated code-quality gates, dependency governance, and a dependency-injected, layered architecture.
